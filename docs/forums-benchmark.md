# Forums page — benchmark

Compared five convening sites (October 2026): [Munich Security Conference](https://securityconference.org/en/), [WEF Annual Meeting](https://www.weforum.org/meetings/world-economic-forum-annual-meeting-2026/), [Aspen Ideas Festival](https://www.aspenideas.org/), [Raisina Dialogue](https://raisinadialogue.org/), [IISS Shangri-La Dialogue](https://www.iiss.org/events/shangri-la-dialogue/). Used as a layout standard, not as a claim of equivalent status.

## What those pages do

| Site | Hero | Edition | What it is | Format | Themes | Invitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MSC | Institution name as the page. Photography is secondary. | Date line immediately under the welcome. | Mandate lives later, as institution copy, not as cards. | Named meeting types (Conference, Leaders Meetings). | Not a tag cloud on the home. | No public signup form on the home. |
| WEF Annual Meeting | The edition is the H1. High-resolution photography, never a stretched graphic. | Date + city as two plain facts. | Long “Overview” paragraph: why this meeting exists. | Implied by “trusted, impartial platform”. | One named theme (“A Spirit of Dialogue”). | Credentialed, not a web form. |
| Aspen Ideas | Festival name. One founder line, then the edition. | Date + place, set as metadata, not a badge stack. | One mandate paragraph. | Format described in prose (walks, pavilion, rooms). | Issues of the day, in sentences. | Contact, not a registration dump on the hero. |
| Raisina | Edition + theme as the identity. | Dates for the coming edition. | One sentence: premier conference on geopolitics and geoeconomics. | Three-day convening; some satellites marked invite-only. | Theme named; not a blog grid. | Invite-only is a fact, not a form. |
| Shangri-La Dialogue | Named summit + edition number. | City + dates. | Open plenary platform for defence debate. | Plenaries plus invitation-only ministerial roundtables. | Session titles are the themes. | Invitations issued to delegations. |

## Shared standard

1. The **forum name is the page**. The section (“Conferences & Forums”) is a breadcrumb, not a second hero.
2. The **edition line is a fact**: month/year and city, in type, not a chip.
3. **Mandate** is one editorial block. **Format** is a short definition list. **Themes** are named, not illustrated as cards.
4. The invitation is a **quiet request**, usually mailto or a later credential. No registration form on the page.
5. Imagery is either real photography or a **sharp mark**. A 1280×720 raster stretched full-bleed fails this test.

## What VisionSeek will not copy

No invented speakers, partners, sponsors, halls, or day-of-month. No blog/news card dump. No MSC/WEF photography we do not have. The Incheon page uses approved wording only, and a vector key visual (Korea–Arabia arc) that stays sharp at any size.
