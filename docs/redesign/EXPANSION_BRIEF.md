# Institutional expansion — operational brief

Owner/executor: Codex. Vision and strategy owner: Dr. Ahmed Abdelalim.
Delivery: this work session, review branch and Vercel preview; production remains a separate review decision.

## Goal
Extend the approved visual direction into eight complete bilingual institutional areas: Work with Us, R&D Opportunities, Programs, Offices, News, Workshops, Careers, About. Preserve the method, fields, Leaders House, reports, contact, legal and Room paths.

## Operating design
A proposed VisionSeek operating model, informed by DARPA's public material, not a claim that VisionSeek has DARPA's staff, funding, procurement authority or delivery record.
- Separate an exploratory idea, a program concept, an approved program and a completed result.
- Each proposed program has a capability, beneficiary, constraint, hypothesis, owner role, tests, phases, stop conditions and transition owner requirement.
- Opportunities explain the contribution sought, evidence expected and how to prepare an expression of interest. No invented awards, budgets or deadlines.
- Offices are proposed functional mandates, with appointment status visible; no fictitious employees.
- Workshops use a weekly six-session cycle with agenda, preparation, facilitator role and measurable artifact. Dates and hosts are unconfirmed until assigned.
- Careers explain roles and expressions of interest, not invented vacancies.
- News separates original VisionSeek editorial notes from attributed external research references.

## Functional outputs
Bilingual section indexes and real detail routes; searchable/filterable directories; section navigation and comprehensive footer; a concept-note preparation form with validated inputs, local Markdown download and explicit email handoff; workshop interest handoff with no fake registration confirmation.

## Acceptance
Every primary and secondary navigation destination resolves in both languages. Lists return useful results and honest empty states. Program-office-opportunity-workshop links remain consistent. No production/DB/DNS/permission changes. TypeScript, lint, build, link validation and browser checks before delivery.
