## Decision and outcome

The founder wants a visible explanation of what VisionSeek is, its place among global institutional models, its distinct approach, and HLO as the path from limited awareness of global possibilities to an institution able to evaluate and apply them. This is a public explanation, not a claim of operating at another firm's scale or an implementation of the program's operating room.

Hypothesis: a prominent homepage entry and one bilingual destination will help a leader explain what VisionSeek does, what HLO delivers, and how to discuss applying it to their institution.

- Check bilingual build/navigation, interactive explanation, contact context and visual layout. Preview only; founder reviews wording before publication.
