# VisionSeek homepage — operational brief

## Outcome
A coherent institutional homepage that makes VisionSeek's opportunity-engineering proposition understandable to government, business and research leaders. Retain MAKE IT POSSIBLE. and the approved logo/fonts. Deliver a reviewable Vercel preview, not a production release.

## Design thesis
Cinematic opening, editorial clarity, disciplined detail. Black and white with a restrained brand-lime accent. One visual scene at a time; no floating tiles or decorative dashboards. Draw on the clarity and ambition of the founder's DARPA/Palantir references without copying their identity or implying equivalent scale, clients or results.

## Hypotheses
- A clear proposition and visible method communicate credibility better than a large collage of technologies.
- A lightweight sequence of existing photographs can deliver atmosphere without an unrelated heavy video or new third-party tracking.
- The homepage should separate fields of exploration from proven projects.

## Scope and ownership
- Executor: ChatGPT — homepage composition, bilingual copy, responsive implementation and validation, this work session.
- Approver: Dr. Ahmed Abdelalim — creative direction and production publication.
- Preserve existing routes, logo, contact destinations and other pages. No database changes.
- Show the method, eight exploration fields, existing Physical AI report, founder and contact path.
- Motion: pause control, manual image selection, reduced-motion support. Static content remains readable without animation.

## Acceptance
- English and Arabic pages render with one H1 and no horizontal overflow at mobile and desktop widths.
- Main navigation, language switch, field/report links and scene controls function.
- Build and lint pass; verify browser layout and image loading.
- No invented clients, metrics, project outcomes or institutional affiliations.
- Provide deployed preview plus explicit validation limitations if any.
