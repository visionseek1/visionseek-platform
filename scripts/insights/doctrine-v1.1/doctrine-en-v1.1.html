THIS IS OUR MOMENT

This is our moment

Korea is not the subject of this report. It is the proofA country that starts as a buyer can become an owner within one working lifetime.

For two generations the Arab world has been an excellent buyer of technology. We bought the best networks, the best power plants and the best devices, and every purchase renewed our dependence. The question was never whether we could afford technology. It was whether we would ever own it.

Korea answers that question. In 1971 it founded its first science and technology university under a special law, with a USAID loan of six million dollars1. In 1983 Samsung developed a 64Kb DRAM, making Korea the third country in the world with advanced VLSI technology4. By 1992 Samsung held the world's largest DRAM market share4. Korea is not the subject of this report. It is the proof that a country that starts as a buyer can become an owner within one working lifetime.

For the first time, several of the conditions behind Korea's leap are present in our region at once. This is the time we have waited for. The danger is that we spend it buying again.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Executive summary: five conclusions

1

Ownership is a sequence, not a purchase.

Korea moved from import to assembly, then absorption, then its own design, then setting the standard. Foreign technology was the first step, never the lastInference.

2

The decisive asset is the "absorption institution."

KAIST was created by special law with an explicit national mission1, and ETRI carried Korea from telephone switches to CDMA to 6G3. Arab states have invested in purchases. Few have built an institution that absorbs what they buyInference.

3

Export is what proves ownership.

The Korean APR1400 reactor counted as owned once it was certified abroad18 and running in the UAE19. Even then, an intellectual-property dispute over its origins had to be settled20Inference (lesson).

4

Settle rights at the door.

Korea's battery leaders ended up before a US trade tribunal over trade secrets17, and its reactor exporter had to settle with Westinghouse20. IP left unresolved when technology is transferred comes back when it is exportedInference.

5

Integrity is infrastructure.

The landmark 2005 Korean stem-cell paper in Science was retracted24. Faked capability destroys trust faster than real capability builds it, so an Arab capability index must measure ownership that has been verified, not announcedInference.

WHY NOW

Why now: what has actually changed

1

National visions with diversification as an explicit goal

Saudi Arabia's Cabinet approved Vision 2030 on 25 April 201625, and the document commits to diversifying the economy26. Egypt launched Vision 2030 in 2016 and has since updated it27. Visions built on purchases stall; visions built on absorption compoundInference.

2

Arab AI institutions now exist

The UAE made AI a national priority in 2017, appointed the world's first Minister of State for AI, and in 2019 announced MBZUAI, described as the first graduate-level, research-based AI university, whose first class started in September 202028. TII is part of Abu Dhabi's Advanced Technology Research Council29. G42 describes itself as "born in Abu Dhabi, operating globally"30. In 2025 Saudi Arabia's PIF launched HUMAIN to work across the AI value chain, including a multimodal Arabic large language model31. These are the first Arab counterparts to Korea's KAIST/ETRI layerInference.

3

The AI window is open

AI capability is still forming, and entry into applied, sector-specific and Arabic-language models costs far less than leading-edge chip fabsInference.

4

Governments are redrawing supply chains

The US CHIPS and Science Act provides $52.7 billion and a 25% investment tax credit, and the White House says the US relies on East Asia for 75% of chip production8. Korea expanded its own semiconductor tax support in 20237. When the leaders subsidize relocation, partners look for new, stable sites rich in energy and capital, and the Arab region is one of themInference.

5

Korea is looking at us

Korea's 6G strategy names the Middle East as a priority region for its digital exports11. A partner that wants our market is a partner that can be asked for absorption termsInference.

6

A Korean ownership proof already stands on Arab soil

Barakah has four APR-1400 units, construction began in 2012, and Unit 1 entered commercial operation in April 202119.

1

Semiconductors

What Korea did
Samsung entered the field by acquiring Hankook Semiconductor in 19744. Its founder made the "Tokyo declaration" on 8 February 19835. That same year it developed the 64Kb DRAM4, having taken basic DRAM technology from Micron under contract, and its trainees had only limited access during the transfer6. In 1992 it developed the industry's first 64Mb DRAM and reached the No. 1 DRAM market share4. ETRI, the state research institute, developed a 4M DRAM in 19893.
Mechanism
License, then absorb by force of necessity when the licensor holds back, then leap ahead in one narrow segment (memory) where manufacturing discipline beats design breadthInference. A state research institute ran in parallel with the firms3Inference (role).
What failed
Market shares are outside the scope of this editionGap. The risks of concentrating on memory, such as price cycles, are an inferenceInference.
Arab transfer
What applies: choose one segment, and write absorption clauses into every licence. What does not: copying a leading-edge fab, because Korea's window of the 1980s is closedInference.
Arab opportunity open now
Packaging and testing, chip design for our own sectors (energy, water), and power semiconductors for the gridInference; needs a feasibility study.

Artificial intelligence

What Korea did
On 24 August 2023 Naver unveiled HyperCLOVA X, its own generative AI, together with the CLOVA X assistant and CUE: search9. It is a domestic platform built on its own data and infrastructure.
Mechanism
A national platform company owns the language, search and data layerInference.
What failed
Outside the scope of this editionGap.
Arab transfer
What applies: language sovereignty, since Arabic is a market large enough to justify models we ownInference. What does not: competing on frontier compute aloneInference.
Arab opportunity open now
Arabic models for health, education, public services, law and heritage. Institutions that can carry this already exist28, 29, 31Inference (opportunity).
Arabic is a market large enough for models we own.
3

Cloud & data

What Korea did
Korea runs a Cloud Security Assurance Program (CSAP) for cloud providers serving the public sector. MSIT and the National Intelligence Service have announced that it will be merged into a single NIS-led verification system, fully in place in the second half of 202712.
Mechanism
The state sets the rules for entering the public market. Sovereignty is exercised through certification, not ownership of every data centreInference.
Arab transfer
A data centre on our soil is use. Owning the operating software, the data standards and the trained engineers is ownershipInference.
Arab opportunity open now
Sovereign-cloud certification that requires local operations and engineering teams, so that every data centre becomes a site of absorptionInference.

5G / 6G

What Korea did
ETRI developed the TDX switch in 1986, commercialized the world's first CDMA mobile system in 1995, and went on to WiBro in 2004 and LTE-Advanced in 20103. On 3 April 2019 Korea achieved the world's first smartphone-based commercial 5G service10. In February 2023 MSIT launched the K-Network 2030 strategy. It runs a preliminary feasibility study for KRW 625.3 billion of 6G R&D, plans a Pre-6G Vision Fest in 2026, and targets a 5G patent share above 30%, up from 25.9% according to IPlytics as cited by MSIT11.
Mechanism
A state lab co-develops with industry, the national operators act as first buyers, and the state then sets the standards agenda3, 11Inference (mechanism).
What failed
Outside the scope of this editionGap.
Arab transfer
What applies: the state as a demanding first customer, with local R&D obligations. What does not: building a full national equipment vendorInference.
Arab opportunity open now
6G is still before standardization, so now is the time to contribute to standards and testbeds rather than buy them later. Korea itself is seeking Middle East partners11Inference.

Robotics

What Korea did
Rainbow Robotics was founded in 2011 by researchers from KAIST's Humanoid Robot Research Center, the team behind Hubo, Korea's first two-legged walking robot. Samsung bought 14.7% in 2023 for KRW 86.8 billion and moved to 35%13. Korea's Fair Trade Commission approved the deal on 5 March 202514.
Mechanism
A university lab becomes a spin-out, which is then acquired by a national champion, closing the capital loopInference.
What failed
Outside the scope of this editionGap.
Arab opportunity open now
Robots for our own hard problems, such as heat, desert logistics, construction, hospitals, and crowd services during Hajj and UmrahInference.

6

Clean energy & batteries

What Korea did
LG Energy Solution dates its lithium-ion research to 1992 and the mass production of cylindrical lithium-ion cells to 199915. LG Chem's own archive dates the start of battery research to 199516, so the two official sources differ. In nuclear, the US NRC certified the APR1400 design with effect from 19 September 201918, and four units were built at Barakah in the UAE19.
What failed
SK and LG fought a battery trade-secret dispute before the US International Trade Commission, which they settled in April 202117. Westinghouse and KEPCO/KHNP settled an IP dispute in January 2025 on confidential terms20. Lesson: IP left unresolved at transfer returns at exportInference.
Arab transfer
Barakah shows that Arab states can host ownership. The next step is to require Arab engineering roles in operation and designInference.
Arab opportunity open now
Grid-scale storage for solar, and processing battery materials where the region has energy and capitalInference.
IP left unresolved at transfer returns at export.
7

Biotech & health tech

What Korea did
The EMA recommended Remsima (Celltrion), together with Inflectra, as the first two monoclonal-antibody biosimilars21, and Remsima was authorised across the EU on 10 September 201322. Samsung Biologics opened its Plant 5 in April 2025, bringing total capacity to 784,000 litres23.
Mechanism
Enter through manufacturing to Western regulatory standards, then move up toward innovationInference.
What failed
The 2005 Science stem-cell paper was retracted24.
Arab opportunity open now
A regional base for biosimilars and vaccine fill-finish serving Arab and African markets, with ethical governance of genomic data within the bounds of Sharia and medical ethicsInference.

A Call to Arab Decision-Makers
A call to Arab decision-makers:
this is our moment.

Korea started its first research university with a six-million-dollar loan1. We have capital, energy, young people and, for the first time, our own AI institutions28–31. What we have lacked is a doctrine: the discipline to turn every purchase into a lesson and every lesson into ownership.

One question for your next technology deal
In your next technology deal, ask one question: what will we own when this contract ends?

If there is no answer, the deal is not finished.

Let the Incheon Dialogue in April 2027 be where we start, and the moment the Arab world stopped buying the future and began building it.
